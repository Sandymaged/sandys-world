الإلغاء

الطلب ممكن يتلغي وهو لسه «قادم»، قبل ما نبدأ تجهيزه، وساعتها بيختفي من حسابكم.

أول ما يبقى «قيد التجهيز»، بتبقوا مسؤولين عن تجهيزه وتسليمه في ميعاده. ولو حصلت أي مشكلة تمنعكم تجهزوه، بلّغونا فورًا، وإحنا هنتكلم مع العميل ونحدد هنعمل إيه حسب سياسة الإلغاء والاسترداد.

تعديل المنتجات

المنتج الجديد بيظهر بعد ما نراجعه، وأي تعديل على منتج منشور (السعر أو العدد أو الصور) بيظهر على طول. والمنتج اللي داخل في باكدج مينفعش يتمسح، كلّمونا الأول نشيله من الباكدج.

المنتج اللي خلص

أول ما العدد يخلص، المنتج (أو اللون أو التركيبة) بيتقفل لوحده ويبان «خلص». علشان كده العدد لازم يبقى مظبوط، لأن أي طلب يوصلكم لازم يتجهز.

مواعيد التسليم

آخر ميعاد مكتوب على كل طلب في حسابكم، وهو قبل يوم التوصيل بيوم. ولو بدأنا تجهيز الطلب في اليوم ده أو بعده، بتسلّموه يوم التوصيل الصبح. ومدة التجهيز اللي بتكتبوها هي اللي بتحدد أقرب يوم توصيل يظهر للعميل.

العيب والتلف

بنشوف كل قطعة وإحنا بنستلمها. لو فيها مشكلة أو مختلفة عن الصورة، بتتبدّل قبل ميعاد التوصيل.

البراند مسؤول عن جودة المنتج، وإنه يكون زي الوصف والصور. ولو فيه عيب أو اختلاف سببه المنتج نفسه، بنتفق مع بعض على التبديل أو رد التمن، ومين يتحمّل التكلفة حسب سبب المشكلة.

Commission

How commission works

Each brand is calculated separately every month, based on its own prices.

Commission-free amount each month= Your average product price × 10

That's the value of 10 pieces at your average price, not your first 10 orders. If your prices are higher, your threshold is higher.

How is the average calculated? We add up the base price of each of your products on the site and divide by the number of products. A product with colours or sizes counts as one product at its base price; the extra for a larger size isn't included. The average comes from product prices, not order values.
For example, with 3 products at 100, 150 and 200: the average is (100 + 150 + 200) ÷ 3 = 150, and the monthly threshold is 150 × 10 = EGP 1,500.

The rules

So everything is clear from the start

Cancellation

An order can be cancelled while it's still «Upcoming», before we start preparing it, and it then disappears from your account.

Once it's «Preparing», you're responsible for preparing it and handing it over on time. If any problem stops you from preparing it, tell us right away, and we'll talk to the customer and decide what to do under the cancellation and refund policy.

Editing products

New products go live after we review them, and changes to a live product (price, stock or photos) show up straight away. A product that's part of a package can't be deleted; talk to us first so we take it out of the package.

Sold-out products

As soon as stock runs out, the product (or colour, or combination) closes automatically and shows «Sold out». That's why stock has to be accurate: every order that reaches you must be prepared.

Hand-over deadlines

The deadline is shown on each order in your account, one day before the delivery day. If we start preparing the order on that day or later, you hand it over on the morning of the delivery day. The preparation time you enter decides the earliest delivery day the customer sees.

Defects and damage

We check every piece when we receive it. If there's a problem, or it's different from the photo, it gets replaced before the delivery date.

The brand is responsible for product quality, and for the product matching its description and photos. If there's a defect or difference caused by the product itself, we agree together on a replacement or refund, and who bears the cost depends on the cause.

Returns and complaints

Talking to the customer and handling complaints is on us, and we're the ones who reply.

Packaging

The product can come in your own packaging or without it, and you can include a small card or sticker with your brand name, but no price or invoice since it's a gift. The outer box and wrapping are in giftful's style, and the customer chooses them on the site.

Money

We collect from the customer. Your share (the price minus commission) is added to your due balance 3 days after the gift is confirmed delivered, and we pay it out every week. Every payout is recorded in your account.

Your share is based on the price the customer paid when ordering, even if you change the price later. If your product is part of a giftful package, you get your share of its full price.

Photos and brand name

We use your name and product photos on the site and on giftful's Instagram, always tagging your brand. If taking photos isn't easy for you, we can help with the photos and touch them up with AI.

Customer data

Orders reach you without the customer's name, number or address. Customers who order through the site are giftful's customers, so we don't contact them outside the site about their orders.
